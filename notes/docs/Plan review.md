# Plan review

**Verdict:** the plan is worth following. Do not treat it as finished.

The sequencing is the valuable part: NISAR access → one dated event → offline change image → then the app. That is the right Space Apps bet.

What the HTML plan did not know: calibrated NISAR L-band PROVISIONAL products have been public since **20 Jul 2026**, for acquisitions from **17 Jun 2026** on. The usable archive is about three months, with a permanent gap **27 Jul – 10 Aug 2026**. A 2024 wildfire is not a NISAR demo.

Search `NISAR_L2_GCOV_PROVISIONAL_V1` or `NISAR_L2_GUNW_PROVISIONAL_V1`, not the placeholder `NISAR_L1_...`. Use at least one real NISAR pair. Sentinel-1 is insurance.
