# Change types

The challenge allows any surface-change type. Pick **one**. Best combo: clear radar signature, well-documented events, achievable in ~7 weeks.

| Option | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Wildfire burn scars** (recommended) | Best for a clear before/after story | Strong radar signature, many recent dated fires, disaster-response angle |
| Flooding | Best for disaster-response framing | Water vs land is very visible in SAR; fast-onset events make dramatic pairs |
| Glacier movement | Harder — extra time | Scientifically rich, but slow and less dramatic in a 3-minute demo |
| Farming / wetland loss | Harder — novelty over drama | Subtler signal; harder to sell to non-expert judges quickly |

Decision still open. Write the pick + event in `decisions/` once chosen. This locks data pull, demo, and pitch. [[Tasks]]
